## My Story — full timeline (from `Journey.jsx`)

1. **School Days — GHSS Kallooppara & NSS Kaviyoor** 📚
   My roots. Schooling at GHSS Kallooppara built my foundation. Then Plus Two at NSS Kaviyoor — honestly, I hated it. The constant pressure, the over-torture to study, the teachers breathing down your neck.

2. **Next Chapter — Mechanical Engineering, Found My People** 🚗 *(Setback)*
   Joined Mechanical Engineering. Loved the college life, the politics, the friendships. Academics suffered — graduated carrying a pile of arrears. Not my proudest moment, but I lived it fully.

3. **Government Attempt #1 — NIA Exam, Cleared It** 📋 *(Almost)*
   Cracked the NIA written exam — proved I could compete when I put my mind to it. Didn't make it through the full process, but passing it gave me a quiet confidence I carried forward.

4. **Government Attempt #2 — Kerala Police Exam, Passed, Then a Broken Leg** 🦵 *(Almost)*
   Cleared the Kerala Police written exam too. But during the physical examination, I broke my leg playing kabaddi. The door slammed shut — not because I wasn't capable, but because life had a completely different plan for me.

5. **Rock Bottom — Breakups, Setbacks & Silence** 🌑 *(Setback)*
   Relationships fell apart. Career felt stuck. Everyone had a plan except me. I had nothing but time — and one open browser tab.

6. **The Turn — Self-Taught: YouTube, Docs & Stubbornness** 💻 *(Turning Point)*
   No bootcamp. No mentor. No CS degree. Just YouTube tutorials, Stack Overflow, and sheer stubbornness. I coded every day until things clicked.

7. **First Win — First Job at a Reputed IT Company** 🚀 *(Win)*
   Landed my first role at a well-known IT company on my own merit. The first day I shipped something to production I knew — this is it. This is where I belong.

8. **Making It Official — Online BCA, Earned While Working** 🎓 *(Win)*
   Enrolled in an online BCA degree while working full-time — shipping code by day, studying by night. The skills were real — the paper was just catching up.

9. **Today — US-Based Company · Bangalore** 🏆 *(Win)*
   Now working at a reputed US-based company in Bangalore. Still the same guy from Kerala who had nothing but YouTube and WiFi — just a lot more commits.
